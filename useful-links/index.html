Government and official alerts

  • Springettsbury Township

    Official township site for East York and Fayfield. Includes a webpage calendar of township events.

  • York County, Pennsylvania

    Official county website for administration, elections, property, emergency services, and parks.

  • York County public emergency alerts

    County page for current public emergency alerts. This website is not an alert service.

  • York County Notify Me

    Official county subscriptions for alerts, news, and calendar notices.

Schools and family resources

  • York Suburban School District

    The district says it serves Spring Garden Township and portions of Springettsbury Township. That is not an exact Fayfield boundary.

  • York Suburban district map

    Official address check. The district says the map is unofficial near borders; use the county parcel tool or registration office for a definitive answer.

Trash, utilities, roads, and property

  • Township recycling and trash guidelines

    Springettsbury Township rules for trash, recycling, yard waste, and bulk items.

  • Penn Waste — Springettsbury Township

    Hauler page for Springettsbury Township collection, including a 2026 collection-calendar PDF.

  • Township leaf collection

    Official 2026 leaf schedule. The township page names East York and Fayfield. Weather can move dates.

  • York County Solid Waste Authority

    County waste-reduction and recycling programs. YCSWA says it is not a trash hauler.

Parks, libraries, and recreation

  • Springettsbury Park

    Township park at the municipal complex: fields, courts, playground, and walking paths.

  • York County Parks and Recreation

    Official county parks department. The page says the county maintains 11 parks and more than 4,300 acres.

  • York County Libraries — find a library

    County library locations. The page says people who live or work in Springettsbury Township can pick up and drop off holds at the township building.

  • PA 211

    Statewide help line and resource directory. York County Human Services also points here.

  • rabbittransit

    Public transit for the York area, including fixed routes and shared-ride booking.
