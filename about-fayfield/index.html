About Fayfield

Fayfield is a neighborhood in East York, Springettsbury Township, York, Pennsylvania. This page does not publish a map or an exact neighborhood line.

Fayfield Community is unofficial and resident-run. It is not an HOA, not the township, and not a government site.

The site is an essential-information hub: a public home for a community calendar, a York-area living guide, useful outside links, newcomer information, and neighborhood history. It is not a Facebook replacement. Discussion stays on Facebook; this site does not host comments or accounts.

This page does not list organizers and does not publish a contact form or email.
