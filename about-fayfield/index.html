About Fayfield

Fayfield sits in East York, in Springettsbury Township. We don’t draw a line on a map here. People who live here already know the streets.

This site is unofficial and resident-run. It isn’t the township, it isn’t an HOA, and it isn’t the government.

We keep a calendar, a few starting points for living in York, some useful links, and what we can honestly say about the neighborhood’s past. Conversation still happens on Facebook. There’s no comment box and no contact form on purpose.

The neighborhood Facebook group isn’t linked yet. We’ll add it when we have the real URL. This is not that group.
