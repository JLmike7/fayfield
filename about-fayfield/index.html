About Fayfield

Fayfield is a small community in Springettsbury Township, in East York. Before the lawns, this was an airfield. Fayfield History has the sourced story.

About this site

Neighbors made this for neighbors. You’ll find a calendar, starting points for living in York, useful links, and a little history. This site is unofficial and resident-run — not the township, an HOA, or the government.
