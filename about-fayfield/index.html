About Fayfield

Fayfield sits in East York, in Springettsbury Township. People who live here already know the streets. We don’t draw a line on a map.

Neighbors keep a calendar here, a few starting points for living in York, some useful links, and what we can honestly say about the neighborhood’s past. Conversation still happens on Facebook.

This site

This site is unofficial and resident-run. It is not the township, an HOA, or the government, and it does not speak for any of them.
